# Missing Channels & Apps Research

This document outlines communication and notification apps that are highly utilized in North America and Europe, but are not yet natively parsed or supported by Conduit.

## Work & Productivity

- **Slack**: Essential workplace communication standard.
- **Google Chat**: Heavily used by businesses on Google Workspace.
- **Zoom**
- **Google Meet**: For meeting alerts and active call notifications.

## General Messaging & Chat

- **Signal**: Extremely popular privacy-focused messenger.
- **Viber**: Highly popular in Eastern Europe and parts of Western Europe.
- **GroupMe**: Heavily used in the US for college groups and sports teams.
- **Skype**: Legacy but continues to see significant enterprise/international use.
- **BlueBubbles**
- **AirMessage**: For users hosting their own iMessage bridges (common in NA).

## SMS / MMS / RCS Replacements

*Alternative texting apps used by those opting out of Google/Samsung Messages.*

- **Pulse SMS**: Popular for cross-device synchronization (texting from tablets/PCs).
- **Chomp SMS**: By the developers of Textra, offering intense customization.
- **QKSMS**: An open-source, ad-free favorite in the privacy/FOSS community.
- **Simple SMS Messenger**: Part of the minimalist "Simple Mobile Tools" suite.

- **Mood Messenger**: Highly customizable with a focus on themes and private boxes.
- **YAATA**: Extremely feature-rich for Android power users.

## Social & Entertainment

- **TikTok**: Massive notification volume (DMs, lives, and content alerts).
- **Twitch**: High engagement notifications (DMs, live streams).
